# One-page brief template

For every person answering customers during peak season, including someone who started this week, usually reading it on a phone. Fill every bracket from the merchant's answers or pasted kit outputs. An unfilled bracket stays as `[DECISION NEEDED: ...]`; never replace it with a guess.

Phone rules:
- No table wider than two columns. Use short lists instead.
- One fact per line. Bullets, not paragraphs.
- Keep it to one page. Cut explanation, not facts.
- Times always with a timezone. Weekdays only when checked against the anchors in SKILL.md.
