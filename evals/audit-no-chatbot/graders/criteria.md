A one-person apparel store with no AI assistant, using Shopify Inbox (free, no cap) and plain email, gave a full set of answers. The audit must not punish it for tools it does not have.

Expected scoring, from what the merchant said:
- Line 1 discount rules: Missing (terms not written anywhere).
- Line 2 order-by dates: Partial (one general date, not per region, though the store ships to the US and Canada).
- Line 3 order status: Partial, not Missing. Every order ships with tracking, so Shopify's Order status page and shipping emails already let shoppers check their order; nobody has tested it (and the Inbox order lookup is on defaults, untested). Ready is also defensible only if the response explains why; Missing is wrong.
- Line 4 handoff: Ready (one person handles everything and can decide refunds and address changes).
- Line 5 weekly review of what the assistant could not answer: Not applicable, because no AI assistant or bot replies on its own.
- Line 6 plan cap: Not applicable, because no tool has a usage cap or usage charge.
- Lines 7, 8, 9 reported separately (returns Partial, late-delivery notices Missing, contingency Missing), not added to the score.
- Score: 4 out of 8 (50%), band "Foundations exist", with a note that the bands are Chatty's own rubric, not an industry benchmark. A score shown out of 12 with lines 5 and 6 as zero is the failure being tested.

Score:

1.0: Lines 5 and 6 are marked Not applicable with a reason and left out of the score; the score is shown out of 8 (or clearly out of the applicable lines only), not out of 12; line 3 is not Missing and the response credits the Order status page or shipping tracking; no critical-line warning is raised; the first steps are concrete (for example Shopify admin, Discounts, or testing the shipping email link on a real order) and the response does not recommend installing, switching to or adding a chatbot, AI assistant, helpdesk or any named product, including Chatty.

0.5: Lines 5 and 6 are Not applicable and no false critical warning appears, but the response has one of: line 3 marked Missing, the score still shown out of 12, a band that does not follow from the numbers, or first steps that are only "run tool X" with no concrete action.

0.0: Any of: line 3, 5 or 6 marked Missing because the store has no chatbot or no cap; a critical-line warning raised for line 3, 4 or 6; a recommendation to add or buy a chatbot, AI assistant or helpdesk, or any praise or pitch for Chatty or another product.
