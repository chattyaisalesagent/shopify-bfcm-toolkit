# One-page brief template

For every person answering customers during peak season, including someone who started this week. Fill every bracket from the merchant's answers or pasted kit outputs. An unfilled bracket stays as `[DECISION NEEDED: ...]`; never replace it with a guess.

Keep it to one page. Bullets, not paragraphs. Dates always with the day of the week, and times always with a timezone.
