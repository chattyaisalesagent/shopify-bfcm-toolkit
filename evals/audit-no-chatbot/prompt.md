Can you score our BFCM readiness? I've put all my answers below so you can score in one go.

We're a small apparel store and I'm the only person doing support. Shoppers reach us through the Shopify Inbox chat on the site and by email. I've never turned on any AI agent or bot, so I reply to every chat and email myself. Inbox is free and our email is just a normal inbox, so nothing we use charges per message or has a limit. We ship physical clothes.

Last BFCM the thing that went wrong was a pile of "where is my order" emails in the first two weeks of December.

- Discount rules: I set up a 20% code in Shopify every year, but the terms aren't written anywhere. I just answer questions as they come.
- Order-by dates: our FAQ says "Order by December 15 for Christmas delivery". One date for everyone, we ship to the US and Canada.
- Order status: every order goes out with a Shopify Shipping label so it has tracking. I've left Inbox on its defaults and never tested anything as a customer.
- Handoff: it's just me. I can refund, change an address, anything.
- Returns: 30-day returns, customer pays return shipping. Nothing about holiday gifts or an extended window.
- Late deliveries: no messages written, I find out when someone emails.
- Contingency plan: nothing written.
