# Roster template

Present the roster in this layout. One row per day across the whole window, weekends included. Do not collapse days into ranges, because days off and weekends make neighbouring days different.

| Date | Day | Phase | Forecast | Hours needed | On shift (shift times) | Hours available | Gap | Uncovered hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-27 | Fri | BFCM week | 220 | 22.0 | Anna 09:00-17:00, Ben 12:00-20:00, Cara 09:00-13:00 | 15.0 | 7.0 | 00:00-09:00, 20:00-24:00 |
| 2026-11-28 | Sat | BFCM week | 198 | 19.8 | Anna 10:00-14:00, Cara 09:00-13:00 | 6.0 | 13.8 | 00:00-09:00, 14:00-24:00 |

Under the table:

**Gap days, largest first.** Date, gap hours, and what would close it in the merchant's own terms: "one more 8-hour person on Saturday 28 November covers 6.0 of the 13.8 hours at 75 percent answering time." Show the arithmetic.

**Per person view.** For each person, the days they work in the window and their total hours, so the merchant can check nobody is scheduled for 20 days straight. Flag anyone working more than six days in a row as `[DECISION NEEDED: <name> works <n> days in a row from <date>, confirm or change]`.

**Uncovered hours.** List only. Do not propose who or what should cover them.

**Seasonal hires.** If the merchant adds a person to close gaps, give them a start date at least a week before their first busy day so they can learn the policies (the Peak Season Playbook brief is written for this).

Mark each gap-closing option as the merchant's choice. Never add a person, move a shift or cancel a day off in the roster without being told to.
